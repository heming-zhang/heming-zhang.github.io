About

I earned my Ph.D. in Biomedical Informatics and Data Science and M.S. in Computer Science at Washington University focused on Agentic AI, Large Reasoning Models (LRMs), and precision medicine. Working with Dr. Fuhai Li and Dr. Michael Province, I developed a biomedical AI ecosystem for multi-omics integration and analysis; and AI Co-Scientist Agent to enable RL-guided knowledge-grounded reasoning for target/drug hypothesis generation.
